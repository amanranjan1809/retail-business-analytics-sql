# Retail Business Analytics — SQL Data Analyst Case Study

## 1. Business Case

This project analyzes retail business data using SQL to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, payment patterns, and operational performance.

The objective is to transform transactional data into actionable business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 2. Business Problem

The business needs to understand:

- How sales and revenue are performing over time
- Which products and categories generate the most revenue
- Which customers contribute the most business value
- How customer purchasing behavior varies
- Which stores and employees perform better
- How payment methods are being used
- Where returns and shipment-related issues occur
- What trends can help improve business performance

---

## 3. Objectives

The main objectives of this case study are:

1. Analyze overall business performance.
2. Identify monthly and yearly revenue trends.
3. Analyze customer purchasing behavior and segmentation.
4. Identify top-performing products and categories.
5. Evaluate store and employee performance.
6. Analyze payment behavior.
7. Examine returns and shipment performance.
8. Use advanced SQL techniques to generate business insights.
9. Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis.

---

## 4. Dataset

The analysis uses a retail business database containing approximately 50,000 records across multiple related tables.

### Main Tables

- customers
- orders
- order_items
- products
- categories
- payments
- employees
- stores
- suppliers
- promotions
- returns
- shipments

The tables are connected through relevant primary and foreign-key relationships and analyzed using PostgreSQL.

---

## 5. Key Business Questions

### Executive Performance
- What are the overall sales, orders, customers and revenue?
- How is business performance changing over time?

### Sales Analysis
- What are the monthly and yearly revenue trends?
- Which months generate the highest revenue?
- How is month-over-month revenue changing?

### Customer Analysis
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- How many customers are one-time, repeat and loyal customers?
- What is the revenue contribution of each customer segment?

### Product & Category Analysis
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories perform best?
- Which products or categories require attention?

### Store & Employee Analysis
- Which stores generate the highest revenue?
- Which employees contribute most to business performance?

### Payment Analysis
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- How much revenue is associated with different payment methods?

### Operational Analysis
- What is the return pattern?
- How are shipments performing?
- Are there areas where operational performance can be improved?

## 6. SQL Techniques Used

The project uses PostgreSQL and demonstrates:

- SELECT statements
- Filtering and sorting
- GROUP BY and HAVING
- Aggregate functions
- JOINs
- Subqueries
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- CASE WHEN
- Window functions
- ROW_NUMBER()
- DENSE_RANK()
- LAG()
- Running totals
- Date and time functions
- Customer segmentation
- Monthly and yearly analysis
